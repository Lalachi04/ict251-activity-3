About Me

My name is Latif Chilala, and I am a Compute science student at Mulungushi University. I am interested in web designing because I enjoy creating attractive and interactive websites. Through Web Technologies, I am learning how HTML organizes content, how CSS improves the appearance of a page, and how JavaScript makes a website respond to people.
